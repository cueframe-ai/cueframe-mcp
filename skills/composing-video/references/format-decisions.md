# Format decisions

Choose these as starting questions, not templates that override the brief.

| Leading material | Useful decision | Evidence that matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product action | Which action and result make the capability understandable? Let the viewer locate the target before the change. | Actual capture when claiming product behavior; legible control, contact and changed state. Reconstructed interface artwork is a different valid medium. |
| Narrated explainer | Which image demonstrates each important statement? Arrange visual changes around phrasing and reading time. | Exact claims/data, actual word timings, intelligibility and captions when requested. |
| Interview or testimony | Which selected passage remains true in its new context? Keep reactions and pauses that carry meaning. | Source ranges and speech continuity; actual speaker identity when reframing. A locked shot can be appropriate. |
| Brand or typography film | What transformation expresses the identity? Type, a logo or color can be the subject. | Glyph form, spacing, visual rhythm, readable holds and intended material; no footage requirement. |
| Illustrated or abstract sequence | What establishes the world, then changes it? Reuse object identity and physical behavior to connect scenes. | Acting, cause and effect, silhouette, continuity and sound relationships. |
| Data or mechanism | What should the viewer understand at each stage? Preserve exact values while staging the explanation. | Factual sources, correct encodings, labels and transformation semantics. |
| Music-led montage | Which musical events warrant visual changes, and which benefit from a hold? | Actual rhythm evidence, picture/sound relationships and the complete phrase. |

For a short social version, consider first-frame clarity and the viewing size without replacing the film's intended opening. For alternate aspects, recompose critical relationships and inspect readability; a crop alone may lose a pointer, face or label. `every-format-from-one-edit` covers delivery variants.

For a launch film, reveal, problem/solution arc, direct demonstration and brand study are distinct options. Do not force every one into the same five-beat narration or add a CTA absent from its purpose. Existing recipe skills provide narrower production details; `video-craft-standards` owns review questions rather than fixed duration, footage-share or score gates.
